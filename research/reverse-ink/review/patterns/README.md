# Pattern review status

The `*-draft.png`, `*-body-overlay.png`, `families.png`, and
`pebble-measurements.json` files record a **rejected** construction. The isolated
pebble contours cross real dark bands and lose their junctions. Do not use the
old assembled SVGs as ink masters. Smooth paths and low selected-gradient
residuals did not establish correct geometry.

Start with `dark-network-correction/comparison.png` for the current correction
of the user-marked Colorless junction. `svg-render.png` renders its actual
positive dark-ink SVG. It is a bounded detail, with three reference comparisons,
not a complete family asset. Its lighter symbol openings are not shared by all
eleven families. Individual signed-edge scores retain uncertainty caused by
photography, registration and foreground text; they are not accuracy ratings.

The wheel and sector-symbol measurement files remain separate, provisional
evidence. They do not validate the rejected surrounding pebble field.

`colorless-network-body/comparison.png` extends the corrected model across the
lower-body window with 41 manually authored light openings in the connected
dark field. This is the current Colorless candidate; its solid and dashed
contours distinguish observed boundaries from text-interpolated paths. The
other ten family bodies remain pending. See the per-source signed-boundary
report for weak contours requiring further inspection, not an approval score.
