Later update: see [five-card revision](FIVE-CARD-REVISION.md) for the current 5 verified / 95 provisional / 26 legacy counts and the four brighter replacements plus Red-Eyes crop. The initial publication report below records the earlier sourcing pass.

Completed 2026-10-03. Scope: original North American 2002 1st Edition LOB-000 through LOB-125.

1. **Strategy.** YGOPRODeck remains the catalog authority. Separate candidate printing observations, reviewer evidence, image-quality checks and preserved original bytes determine visual sourcing. Seller titles, filenames and artwork matching are discovery aids only. Every front remains local at runtime.

2. **Priority followed.** Verified native exact scan > matching market/product image > recoverable graded archive > collector listing > YGOPRODeck. Printing and quality eligibility precede priority and weighted ranking. Selected replacements: one Face to Face scan (LOB-123), one eBay product photo (LOB-119), one clean graded-card crop (LOB-090), and 97 eBay collection fronts. The latter 99 remain explicitly provisional because individual North American evidence is missing; no candidate gains verified status from resolution alone.

9. **Upscaling: 0.** No AI, generative fill, denoise, sharpening, recoloring, missing-pixel reconstruction or baked holographic effect. Natural print dots, wear and source coloration remain. Lossless runtime PNGs retain original resolution conservatively; this increases repository and download size.

10. **Provenance.** Schema 2 per-card JSON separates `catalogIdentity`, `printingIdentity`, `imageProvenance`, `manualReview` and ranked `candidates`. It includes source page/image URLs, observed printing, claimed printing, regional evidence, original/runtime resolutions and SHA-256 hashes, normalization coordinates, quality scores and caveats. Files: `public/cards/yugioh/lob-first-edition/LOB-NNN.json`, `sources.json`, `manual-review.json`. Acquisition/review manifest: `scripts/lob-fronts/candidates.json`; discovery and rejected-source ledger: `scripts/lob-fronts/research.json`. Unknown evidence fails closed; changed original bytes invalidate review.

11. **Local assets.** Originals: `research/yugioh-lob/fronts/LOB-NNN/`, with paired/shared backs preserved. Replacements: `public/cards/yugioh/lob-first-edition/fronts/LOB-NNN.png`. Registered foil/title/stamp PNG masks: `public/cards/yugioh/lob-first-edition/maps/`. Existing baseline JPGs remain intact. Review overlays/contact sheets and viewer captures: `artifacts/lob-fronts/` (local QA, excluded from Git).

12. **Source limitations.** TCGplayer generic product imagery did not verify individual exact prints. Cardmarket returned 403 and carries regional variants. PSA auction details required sign-in; an accessible Basic Insect entry was Australia. CGC images were dynamic/inaccessible. Heritage image access was unavailable. One indexed Face to Face Right Leg scan returned 404. eBay titles sometimes contradicted the pixels, and some item URLs redirected to generic product pages. Full source-specific URLs/outcomes are recorded in the research ledger.

13. **Printing mismatches and rejected photos.** A Dark Magician listing actually showed Portuguese MAGO NEGRO, LDB-P005. A Man-Eater Bug listing showed Unlimited with a silver stamp. PSA Basic Insect was Australia. The three YGOPRODeck baselines show modern replica/general printing features. Additional photos were rejected for low native card resolution, glare, sleeve reflections, unreadable stamps or baked foil reflections; a paired-back Reaper listing still failed the resolution/reflection gate. Rejection reasons remain visible.

14. **Validation.** Build passes. All 12 Python policy/normalization/publication tests and both new LOB asset/provenance tests pass. All 126 local fronts decode in the live WebGPU viewer; 10 representative cards render at three angles with no page errors or remote image requests. Colored edge overlays, normalized contact sheets, all 20 foil-title masks and live Armored Starfish were inspected. Catalog metadata/identities and rarity match the prior catalog. Broader tests previously recorded 228/230 passing, with unrelated Base Set identity and Wizards promo baseline failures. Concurrent TCGL work later introduced an extensionless import that blocks the existing Node Yu-Gi-Oh suite; the new asset tests are isolated from that unrelated runtime dependency and pass.

15. **Runtime changes.** Optional provenance/status fields were added to Yu-Gi-Oh source types, and LOB front/source references and PNG map registrations were updated. The existing fallback label is reused. No changes were made by this work to shaders, material behavior, Secret Rare maps/rework, rarity logic, pack collation or pack browser. The offline fetch/publish workflow is documented in `scripts/lob-fronts/README.md`.
