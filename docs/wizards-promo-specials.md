# Wizards numbered promos 18, 33, 50–53

The user's correction identifies 18 Team Rocket's Meowth and 33 Scizor as non-holo prints. Both use the complete TCGdex front in the print-only viewer profile. The sparkle and diagonal texture visible in the scans remain printed pixels; there are no foil maps or generated relief.

Promo 50 Celebi, 51 Rapidash, 52 Ho-oh, and 53 Suicune retain their complete e-Reader-era fronts and dot strips. Celebi and Suicune also retain their printed Pokémon 4Ever marks. The set catalog contains all numbered 1–53 with no booster IDs. A distinct Ancient Mew entry follows numbered 53 in the promo browser and gallery and opens the existing authored Ancient Mew card.

`special-sources.json` records TCGdex image and metadata URLs and hashes for 18, 33, and 50–53. The unmarked 51–53 source prints were chosen where TCGdex also lists separate Pokémon Center stamped variants.
