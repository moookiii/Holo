# Jolteon ex 153/131

The exact printing uses eleven dedicated 1800x2475 PNG maps and the established
Prismatic SIR material. All five supplied references are retained and hashed
in 153-holo-evidence.json. The etching master is now available locally.

The green (#22B14C) and burgundy (#880015) user outlines define the registered
body and crystal masks. The small jaw opening stays background; the forehead
between the front jewels retains body texture. Crystal coverage is combined
with the shared silver bar mask, moved to Jolteon's ex title. The shared
retreat symbols are removed because this printing has no retreat cost.

The master photo guides curved diagonal background incisions, gentler body
texture, perimeter relief and the bottom-right microdiamond field. No print
brightness or random noise creates height. Continuous relief is differentiated
before material masking to avoid artificial raised cutout boundaries.

Each attack energy has concentric grooves centered on its printed circle:
(58,494), (94,494), (58,618), (94,618), (130,618), in 600x825 coordinates.
Black center symbols remain protected and the discs do not use microdiamonds.
Rules panels and individually enclosed text glyphs also remain smooth.

Groove spacing/depth, hidden continuation, and the feathered extent of the
bottom-right diamond field are estimates from the 571x800 reference photo.

Regenerate: python scripts/prismatic/jolteon_cutouts.py, then
python scripts/prismatic/jolteon_maps.py. The maps generator consumes the
registered PNG masks. Review overlays and four-light/eight-angle captures
are in artifacts/jolteon-153/.
