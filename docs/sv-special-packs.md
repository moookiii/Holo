# SV special expansion openings

Paldean Fates, Shrouded Fable, Black Bolt and White Flare now have separate English retail recipes and exact local TCGL card pools. Each ordinary pack has four commons, three uncommons, two reverse/replacement slots, one rare slot and a final Basic Energy (11 collectible cards; the online code is not a collectible card). No cross-expansion substitutions are permitted. Existing etched surfaces are unchanged.

Paldean Fates uses TCGplayer's measured rates: shiny rare 25.44%, shiny ultra rare 7.72% in the first reverse; IR 7.22%, SIR 1.72%, hyper rare 1.61% in the second; double rare 15.89% and ultra rare 6.61% in the rare slot.

Shrouded Fable uses Danny Phantump's 1,008-pack opening: ACE SPEC 58, IR 82, SIR 15, hyper rare 7, double rare 170, ultra rare 72. ACE SPEC replaces the first reverse; IR/SIR/hyper replace the second.

Black Bolt and White Flare use TCGplayer's combined 720-pack sample: Poké Ball 30.56%, Master Ball 5.14%, IR 16.39%, SIR 1.25%, double rare 21.11%, ultra rare 5.83%. Both Ball variants occupy the first reverse (unlike Prismatic); Master Ball is Pokémon only. The expansions retain separate checklists. Basic Energy fronts are SVE 017–024. Stellar Crown through Destined Rivals use 009–016; earlier releases use 001–008, preserving the existing 151 Cosmos Energy exception.

Rates are empirical estimates, not official odds. Slot outcomes are sampled independently and eligible cards uniformly within each tier; print-sheet sequencing, per-card frequencies and correlations have not been measured. BWR, God-pack and foil Basic Energy frequencies are unknown and are not fabricated for ordinary random openings.

White Flare offers a separately labeled fixed God pack replay from Jonsandman's physical opening at 12:56–15:10. It restores sealed order after the opener's Energy trick: Heatmor 104, Scrafty 139, Lillipup 154, Sewaddle 087, Druddigon 151, Tepig 096, Vanillite 111, Stoutland 156, Mienfoo 133, Whimsicott ex 165 and foil Metal Energy SVE 024. This replay does not imply a probability. Other special-pack sequences are not modeled without verified contents.

Ten original English wrapper fronts were cropped offline without rotation or stretching. Source URLs, crop coordinates, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `public/packs/pokemon/sv-special-wrapper-sources.json`. Wrapper reverse artwork remains the existing fallback because matching back scans were not available.

Non-booster catalogs have explicit distribution labels: SVP promos, alternate printings and Energy have no standalone randomized booster. My First Battle is a fixed-deck product (two 17-card mini decks), released September 29, 2023, between 151 and Paradox Rift. The SV picker preserves release chronology, including these catalogs.
