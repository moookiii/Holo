# Victory Cup and unreleased English BW promos

User-confirmed physical finishes: Victory Cup BW29 is 3rd place (bronze), BW30
2nd place (silver), and BW31 1st place (gold). All three use the existing rainbow
`pokemon-crosshatch` material on their own TCGL plates. Rank/trophy colors stay
in the unchanged clean fronts; no separate bronze/silver/gold foil shader is added.
The supplied BW29 Spring 2013 photograph shows crossed spectral bands across
the frame, rules area, trophy, ribbons and confetti, with Victini excluded.

Original source whiteplates supply continuous card-specific coverage and print
exclusions. Offline registration uses the existing horizontal Unity UV domain
[145/1024, 878/1024], full height, bilinear sampling to 733x1024 PNG, no vertical
flip. No normal or height map is authored. The existing Crosshatch material,
lighting response, pattern scale and roughness remain unchanged. Full-card
source comparison and per-card source/output hashes are retained. Regenerate
with `scripts/tcgl/prepare_bw_victory.py`; runtime overrides survive import.

TCGL long-form variant IDs remain unavailable for these supplemental entries.
Original material manifests call this Tinsel, while the user identifies the
physical rainbow Crosshatch. The user-selected family takes priority; source
identities are retained honestly without inventing export IDs. The BW29 photo
is not a calibrated multi-angle measurement, and does not establish exact
angular intensity for every BW30/BW31 copy.

Pikachu BW77 and Raichu BW78 are unreleased English promos, as identified by
the user. Their existing digital fronts remain inspectable as print-only with
an explicit unreleased-English label. There is no physical English finish to
reproduce, and these entries are removed from the unfinished holo backlog.
