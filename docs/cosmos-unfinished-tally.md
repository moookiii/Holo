Audit date: October 12, 2026. Current working-tree registrations and PNG assets; counts are distinct card/variant printings, not distinct Pokémon.

| **Total** | **135** |

## Counting criteria

Unfinished means the exact Cosmos printing remains `print-only`/deferred, or its actively bound motif PNG is entirely black. Completed XY overrides in `CompletedCosmosSurfaces.ts` are applied before counting. SM populated maps agree with the 56 published registrations. BW has 31 active authored printings and 17 deferred printings. Neo Revelation has 16 empty bound motif maps, including Shining Magikarp and Shining Gyarados under the current Cosmos registration.

Excluded: 73 completed XY overrides; 56 authored SM printings; 31 authored BW printings; existing vintage cards with populated Cosmos maps; and the user-approved shared-sheet treatment for 193 SV, 148 SWSH and 19 MEP printings. Shared-sheet completion does not claim exact physical-copy placement. The Prismatic pack-specific Basic Energy reverse definitions retain their existing smooth procedural Cosmos treatment; these are separate context bindings of energy cards already covered by the approved SV shared-sheet inventory, not additional unfinished catalog printings.

This is an implementation/placement backlog audit, not a new visual certification. Nonempty maps do not prove that every faint physical feature or angular response is fully calibrated. Stale availability labels and unused legacy empty PNGs are not counted. No card assets or material definitions changed.
