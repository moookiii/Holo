# Glaceon ex 150/131

The picker entry `pokemon:sv08.5-150:holo` uses ten dedicated 1800x2475 PNG
maps. The two supplied angled masters and crown close-ups guide the ice-panel,
scroll and perimeter incisions. The separate cutout master guides Glaceon's
body, head flaps, ear and curled tail; the clean front supplies coordinates.

The supplied silver mask is preserved as a reference and registered to this
card's ex title and single retreat icon. Individual jewels, connected pale
crown stalks, colored tips and diamond ornaments extend its microdiamond
coverage. Lower-left background microdiamonds have a separate feathered map
and retain the underlying etched normals. Character texture uses fine relief,
without a character sparkle layer.

Foil coverage, print protection and relief are separate. Text protection follows
individual glyphs, with dedicated smooth rule panels. Continuous geometric
relief is differentiated before clipping, avoiding artificial raised cutout
edges. The approved material supplies ink-colored metallic highlights under
direct light and a uniform grating responding to authored normals.

Spacing, depth, hidden line continuation, microdiamond density and the soft
extent of lower-left coverage are estimates from the photos, not measured
manufacturing data. Print brightness is never used as height.

Regenerate with `python scripts/prismatic/glaceon_maps.py`. Source and map
hashes are recorded in `150-holo-evidence.json`. Colored registration overlays
and the four-light/eight-angle review are in `artifacts/glaceon-150/`.
