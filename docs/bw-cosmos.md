# BW Cosmos physical-copy placement

29 of the 48 imported Cosmos printings now use the unchanged `pokemon-base-set-2-cosmos` profile and `BaseSet2Cosmos.ts` renderer. Each uses its own stable placement, authored offline as a 1200x1650 antialiased grayscale PNG in the clean front's 600x825 full-card coordinates. Seed affects optical orientation only. No dots are randomly placed, no runtime image detection occurs, and no shader or other series was changed.

The active printings are Raichu 40/99, Salamence 8/20, Riolu BW33 alternate, and promos BW07, BW08, BW10, BW12, BW18, BW26, BW34, BW56, BW57, BW58, BW59, BW64, BW66, BW67, BW76, BW80, BW87, BW88, BW89, BW90, BW91, BW92, BW93, BW94, BW97 and BW98. Source identity, exact long-form TCGL variant, original image hash, projective transform, continuous motif geometry and output hash are in `scripts/tcgl/bw-cosmos/registration.json` and each card's evidence JSON.

Photos/scans of the exact English print are registered by SIFT print anchors and RANSAC to the unchanged clean TCGL master. Automatic blob proposals remain separate and inactive. `corrections.json` rejects all proposals by default and explicitly measures only reviewed physical features. Printed bubbles, sparks, water strokes, flowers, oval patterns, lightning, ice shards and subject highlights were compared with the clean master and excluded. Whole filled discs preserve large spots; measured diamonds, crosses and beveled silhouettes use explicit contours. Subject occlusion stays in the original TCGL whiteplate, independently of the motif PNG.

Own byte-exact `original-foil.png` sources are preserved. The established Unity UV box `[145/1024,0,878/1024,1]` registers each source to 733x1024 without vertical flip. `foil-controls.png` retains the registered source channels and `foil.png` its luminance. This whiteplate already contains exact opaque print and foil-window boundaries, so a separate zero protection PNG avoids attenuating those boundaries twice. No supplied SAM map was replaced. Material roughness comes from the unchanged Cosmos profile; no artwork brightness, relief, rainbow replacement material or extra glitter was introduced.

`author_bw_finishes.py` has separate `propose`, `draft`, `publish` and `activate` steps. `activate` changes reviewed BW records only. Ordinary import reads the stable registry and preserves authored motifs. Physical source acquisition is recorded in `sources.json`; explicit alternate-photo choices are in `extra-references.json`. Original physical images and registered copies are retained under `research/tcgl/bw-cosmos/`.

Full-card physical boundary overlays, clean-front overlays and motif-only PNGs are saved under `artifacts/bw-finishes/cosmos/<card-id>/`. Live captures for all 29 cards, the two gold cards, Espeon 155, Sylveon 156 and Base Set 2 Alakazam use identical Studio, Holo skim, tilt, Low key, Strip and Moving light controls, plus three fixed moving-light azimuths. `artifacts/bw-finishes/live/report.json` reports no page errors or failed local asset requests. Large discs remain continuous while individually changing response, and print stays opaque.

This is visible-feature placement, not a claim that every faint spot has been recovered. JPEG grain and unresolved subpixel features are omitted; some dense cross fields remain incomplete. Flat photos do not measure optical depth or fully establish the angular response of the physical sheet. Different copies have different placements. 19 remaining printings retain empty pending motif PNGs and print-only optics because the available images cannot reliably separate their physical foil boundaries from printed detail. The explicit remaining list is `docs/bw-cosmos-review.json`; activating random or printed highlights would misrepresent completion.

Validation: six targeted tests pass across BW integration, registered Cosmos and BW ball reverse coverage. The live renderer opens every active Cosmos variant and records nine controls per card. Unrelated sets and existing Cosmos material/pattern code are unchanged.

Flareon BW88 uses the replacement PSA 8 scan, certification 143180070, with thirty manually measured discs and one beveled feature across its visible foil field. Large discs remain complete; printed botanical circles stay excluded. The earlier single-disc photo was rejected as insufficient and remains inactive in `rejected-references.json`. Faint grain and features obscured by print remain unresolved.

Leafeon BW87 (PSA 8 95141728), Vaporeon BW89 (PSA 7 105241605) and Jolteon BW91 (PSA 7 100411710) use new field-revealing physical scans. Their 59, 53 and 58 measured discs are copy-specific; printed circles, flowers, yellow chains and geometric facets were excluded during clean-master review. Small unresolved grain remains omitted.

Umbreon BW93 uses PSA 7 130126221, with 55 measured discs and four observed angular perimeters. Its large disc and clipped edge feature retain their own boundaries. Printed stars, crescents and round ornamentation stay excluded. Dense faint particles remain unresolved.
