# 151 Ultra Rare optical correction

Scope: the sixteen 151 Ultra Rares (182–197), using their existing printing-specific fronts, foil coverage, protection, roughness and offline TCGL normals. No site image, mask, rarity or set data was changed. IRs, Double Rares, SIRs and other sets keep their existing optics.

The original generic grating occupied a broad angular range, while the gallery omitted the focus renderer's etched-ink reflection. The initial correction made diffraction more selective but lacked the IR-like diagonal sheet behavior requested by the user. The final shader reserves 28% of the Ultra Rare diffraction strength for `illustrationRareReflection`: effective strength 0.1344, approximately 7.5% of the standalone IR strength 1.8. The remaining spectral contribution retains its reflective angular envelope. Both contributions follow the exact authored normal and pass through the ink filter. The metallic ridge term uses the reference 150-exponent lobe and ink color, with no added white floor. No extra emboss or generated relief is introduced.

The user-supplied `0000007982.mp4` is the physical reference; its approximately 3–7 second segment shows Ultra Rare Zapdos 192. The earlier eBay reference was discarded at the user's request. The video shows localized diagonal color, neutral metallic intervals and concentrated highlights; exposure and the protective holder limit physical calibration.

`151-ultra-evidence.json` records all exact TCGL variant IDs, source URLs, dimensions and hashes. Examples are `Charizardex_sv3-5_183_std_UltraRare_SunPillar_Etched` and `Zapdosex_sv3-5_192_std_UltraRare_SunPillar_Etched`. Sources are 733×1024 front/etch and 367×512 foil. The existing 1800×2475 normals were verified pixel-exact against the reference inversion, full-domain bilinear resize, Scharr 1/32, gain 1.03 and post-derivative protection method. No flip, crop or offset; no assets replaced. Untouched sources are also preserved locally under `research/tcgl/<card-id>/`.

Both views call `UltraRareLayer.ts`. The experimental full-resolution gallery path is preserved but disabled by `ULTRA_RARE_FULL_RES_GALLERY = false`, per the user's performance preference. The gallery uses its existing shared atlases and batching. This preserves the optical formula but loses fine etch detail at the gallery's existing 128×180 normal resolution; exact pixel parity is not claimed.

Validation: TypeScript passed and six focused tests passed, including original hashes for all sixteen Ultra Rares. `scripts/151-ultra-review.mjs` completed on WebGPU with no page/shader errors for Charizard 183, Zapdos 192 and Blastoise 184: neutral, shallow tilt, reflection, grazing, 17 rotation poses, low key, strip light, nine moving-light phases, actual gallery and matched viewer/gallery poses. Espeon 155 and Sylveon 156 reference captures use the same neutral Studio controls. Captures are in `artifacts/151-ultra/ir-blend/`; the sampled moving-light preview is `zapdos-moving-light.webp`. Reported sampled frame time was about 5.55–5.58 ms, not a cold-load benchmark. Bright specular angles remain bright; physical depth and exact real-world radiometry remain uncalibrated.
