1800x2475 PNG maps. Two supplied angled photos guide the curved flower etching;
the separate cutout photo guides the character boundary. The additional crown
close-up guides the connected crystal base, colored tips, reflective eyes and
small diamond ornaments. The clean local front provides registration only.

The shared silver microdiamond source is preserved unchanged in research. The
generated trim mask removes the evolution portrait ring, registers the shorter
ex title, extends the Basic-card top bar and keeps Eevee's single retreat icon.

Fine body relief is clipped to the ears, face, collar, torso, paws and tail.
The character has no sparkle layer. Individual printed letters and rule panels
are protected independently of the surrounding pale flowers.

material masking, then softly blended to avoid cutout rims and hard arc seams.
The perimeter receives fine curved etching. Ink-colored reflection comes only
from direct lights, using the approved Prismatic lighting model. Crown cuts are
smaller and denser to follow the supplied close-up.

Groove spacing, depth, hidden line continuation and material constants are visual
estimates from the references, not recovered manufacturing measurements.

reference policy are in `167-holo-evidence.json`. Review overlays and rendered
lighting comparisons are in `artifacts/eevee-167/`.
